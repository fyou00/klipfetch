## Controls
- Primary: `#BF3909` fill · `#FFFDF9` ink · `4px` radius · `48px` height (`<5%` viewport footprint)
- Secondary: `#FFFCF7` fill · `1px #1A1715` or `#877D71` border · `4px` radius · `34–44px` height
- Surfaces: flat `#F5F2EC` canvas → `2px #1A1715` heavy rules & `1px #D8D0C4` hairlines → `#ECE7DD` recessed wells (`1px` border) → `#FFFCF7` elevated sheets (inputs, nav bar, dialogs only)

## Motion
- Character: state-only (`120ms` micro, `220ms` short); zero decorative reveal, zero bounce, zero shadows.
